Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. General Policy

Support payments are voluntary contributions intended to support LORD ZORO's creator activities. Because payments are voluntary, refund requests are reviewed individually and are not automatically guaranteed.

2. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you accidentally completed the same support payment more than once, contact us with the relevant Razorpay payment identifier, transaction amount, date, and the name or gamertag used during checkout. Duplicate-payment cases may be reviewed for a refund.

3. Unauthorized Payments

If you believe a transaction was unauthorized, contact your payment provider promptly and also contact LORD ZORO through Telegram with the available transaction information. Do not send complete card details, PINs, passwords, or other payment credentials.

4. Failed or Interrupted Payments

If money appears to have been debited but the payment does not appear to have completed successfully, first allow the payment provider's transaction status to update. If the issue remains unresolved, contact support with the transaction or payment identifier so the payment can be investigated.

5. Refund Review

Where a refund is approved, the refund will generally be initiated through the applicable payment-processing channel and may take additional time to appear in the original payment method. Processing times can vary based on the payment provider and financial institution.

6. Information Required

To investigate a refund request, we may ask for the Razorpay payment identifier, order identifier, transaction amount, transaction date, name or gamertag, and a brief explanation of the issue. Never provide your card PIN, UPI PIN, password, or full card credentials.

7. Contact

For refund questions, contact LORD ZORO through Telegram. Please keep your payment identifier available when requesting transaction assistance.
