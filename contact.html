About LORD ZORO Billing

LORD ZORO provides an online billing and payment interface designed to make payments and billing-related interactions simple and convenient for our customers.

Customers may use the billing page to review the applicable payment information and proceed with an available payment method through the payment service provided at checkout.

Our billing page is designed to provide a clear and secure experience while keeping payment processing separate from the main website interface.

🔐 Payment Security

Protecting customer payment information is important to us. LORD ZORO uses third-party payment processing services to process online payments rather than handling sensitive card information directly through the public billing page.

  • Payments are processed through the payment gateway available at checkout.
  • Sensitive payment credentials such as card numbers and banking authentication information are not intended to be collected through our public contact page.
  • Payment processing is handled by the applicable payment service provider according to its security procedures.
  • Server-side payment credentials and API secrets are kept separate from browser-side public website code.
  • Our website is hosted using HTTPS-enabled infrastructure, helping protect information transmitted between your browser and the website.
  • Customers should always verify that they are using the official LORD ZORO website before entering payment or personal information.
Security notice: LORD ZORO will never ask you to send your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP, banking password, or payment authentication credentials through Telegram or email.

💳 Billing & Payment Support

Payment completed but service not received?

Contact us using the email address or Telegram account above. Please provide your payment reference or transaction details where appropriate so that we can investigate the issue.

Payment failed?

If your payment was unsuccessful, check the payment status shown by the payment provider and try again if necessary. If money was deducted but the transaction did not complete, contact us with the relevant transaction information.

Duplicate payment?

If you believe you have accidentally made a duplicate payment, contact our support team with the relevant transaction references so the payment can be reviewed.
