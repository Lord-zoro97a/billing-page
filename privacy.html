Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the LORD ZORO support and payment website. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to provide the support-payment experience and respond to payment-related questions.

2. Information You Provide

The website may collect the name or gamertag you enter when creating a payment order and any optional note you submit. You should avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not required for payment support.

3. Payment Information

The website does not intentionally collect or store your complete card number, card security code, UPI PIN, or banking password. Payment details are entered into the Razorpay checkout experience and processed by Razorpay according to its applicable policies.

4. Transaction Information

Payment-related information may include the amount, Razorpay order identifier, payment identifier, transaction status, and information necessary to investigate payment issues or respond to support requests.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to create and process payment orders, confirm transactions, provide customer support, investigate failed payments, address refunds, prevent abuse, maintain website security, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

6. Razorpay and Other Service Providers

Payment processing is provided through Razorpay. The website may also use third-party infrastructure or services required to host, display, secure, or operate the website. Those providers may process information under their own policies and contractual obligations.

7. Telegram

The website provides a Telegram contact link for support. If you contact LORD ZORO through Telegram, information shared through that service is subject to Telegram's applicable terms and privacy practices.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The basic website is designed to function without requiring an account. Third-party services, payment checkout, hosting infrastructure, or embedded resources may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access or misuse. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Your Choices

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for transaction records, support, security, dispute handling, accounting, or legal obligations. You may contact us regarding privacy questions or requests relating to information associated with a support transaction.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns, contact LORD ZORO through Telegram. Please do not send passwords, PINs, full card details, or other payment credentials through Telegram or the website.
